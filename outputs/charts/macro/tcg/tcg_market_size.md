## Context

**EN**
- The 10% CAGR baseline rests on a concentrated supply side. Pokemon together with Bandai Namco, Hasbro, Square Enix, and Tomy held roughly 55% of the market in 2025, so growth in the headline figure largely tracks the release calendars of these few publishers.
- Digital extensions are pulling forward the curve. Pokémon TCG Pocket revenue climbed from $66.2M in January 2025 to $90.4M in February 2025, with digital companion products converting print-product demand into recurring spend.
- Estimates vary widely across research firms, from $8.4B to $13.3B for the 2025 baseline, reflecting differences in scope (collectibles only vs. games plus accessories) more than directional disagreement. All major reports project double-digit CAGRs through 2031.
